## Why the copy says what it says

Every feature named here was checked against the shipped Activities — feeds, collections,
private saves, groups, direct messages with message requests, hashtags, search, 2FA, session
management, block/mute, hidden words, in-app account deletion. Nothing is aspirational; Play
rejects listings that describe features the app does not have.

The **"About permissions"** section is deliberate and should not be trimmed. The app declares an
Accessibility Service, which draws extra scrutiny, and Play's Prominent Disclosure requirements
expect the store listing to explain it. Stating plainly that the service exists only to detect
screenshots, that the app never reads screen contents, and that nothing is uploaded without an
explicit user action, pre-empts the most likely reason for rejection.

The three legal URLs at the end are live and were verified serving in both languages.

## Assets still needed (not text — cannot be generated here)

| Asset | Spec |
|---|---|
| App icon | PNG/JPEG, 512 × 512, ≤ 1 MB |
| Feature graphic | PNG/JPEG, 1024 × 500, ≤ 15 MB |
| Phone screenshots | 2–8, PNG/JPEG, ≤ 8 MB each, 16:9 or 9:16, each side 320–3840 px |

Use **at least 4 screenshots at 1080 px minimum** on the short side — below that you are excluded
from Play's promotional placements.

Suggested screenshots, in order: the capture/detect prompt, the For You feed, a post with
comments, Collections or Cloud Save, a group, and the privacy settings screen. Lead with capture,
since that is the app's actual differentiator.

Localised graphics are optional — Play falls back to the default language's images when a
translation has none.
