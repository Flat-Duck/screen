Akukas turns the screenshots you already take into something you can actually find, keep and share.

Most screenshots disappear into a crowded gallery and are never seen again. Akukas notices the moment you take one, offers to act on it right away, and gives it a place to live — privately in your own library, or publicly with people who care about the same things you do.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CAPTURE THE MOMENT YOU TAKE IT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Akukas detects a new screenshot as soon as it is saved and offers to edit, save or post it
• Take a screenshot from anywhere with the Quick Settings tile — no need to open the app
• Crop, mark up and clean an image before you share it

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
KEEP IT PRIVATE, OR SHARE IT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Cloud Save keeps screenshots in a private space only you can see
• Collections let you group what you keep — receipts, recipes, design ideas, anything
• Post to your timeline when you do want to share, with a caption and alt text
• Archive, restore or permanently delete anything you have posted

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
A FEED WORTH FOLLOWING
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Two feeds: the people you follow, and a For You feed built from the topics you pick
• Follow hashtags and explore what is trending
• Comment, reply, like, repost and save what you find
• Search people, posts and tags
• Tell the feed to show fewer posts like a given one — it listens

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
GROUPS AND MESSAGES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Create or join groups around a shared interest and share screenshots into them
• Invite people directly, and manage the invitations you receive
• Direct messages, with message requests so strangers cannot land in your inbox uninvited

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
CONTROL THAT ACTUALLY WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• A private account, if you want one — approve every follower yourself
• Block and mute anyone; hide words you would rather not see
• Decide who can comment on and interact with your posts
• Two-factor authentication, and a list of every active session you can revoke
• Delete your account and its content from inside the app, whenever you want

Akukas is fully available in Arabic and English, including right-to-left layout throughout, plus a dark theme.

ABOUT PERMISSIONS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Akukas asks for an Accessibility Service so it can tell when you take a screenshot. That is the entire purpose of the app, and it is the only reason the permission is used — Akukas does not read the contents of your screen, and it never uploads a screenshot on its own. A screenshot leaves your device only when you choose to post or cloud-save it.

A foreground service keeps detection running reliably in the background, and text recognition runs on your device rather than on our servers.

We do not sell your personal data.
