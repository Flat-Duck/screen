# Privacy Policy — Akukas App

**Last Updated: August 27, 2026**

Your privacy is our top priority. This Privacy Policy outlines what data the Akukas application collects, how we use it, with whom we share it, and your rights regarding your data. By using the application, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

## 1. Information We Collect
## 1.1. Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Data:** Name, username, email address or phone number, password (encrypted), and profile picture.

- **Published Content:** Screenshots and images you choose to post, comments, attached descriptions, and interactions (likes, shares, saves).

- **Cloud-Saved Content:** Screenshots you choose to upload to your private space via the Cloud Save feature (see Section 4).

- **Communications with Us:** Messages and reports you submit to our support team.

## 1.2. Information Collected Automatically
- **Device Data:** Device type and manufacturer, operating system version, unique device identifier, and time zone.

- **Device Language:** We read your phone's system language to automatically configure the app interface and customize content appropriate to your culture and region. This requires no special permissions and reveals no personal information.

- **Usage Data:** Features accessed, launch times, content interactions, crash reports, and performance metrics.

- **Network Data:** IP address and connection type.

## 1.3. Access to Screenshots on Your Device
This is the core functionality of the application, which we outline with complete transparency:

- The app accesses the **Screenshots folder** on your device (and not your full photo gallery, unless explicitly granted access) to detect new screenshots as soon as they are taken.

- Upon taking a screenshot, the app displays a floating button offering two immediate choices: share the shot to the **Timeline** (the public feed where users worldwide share interesting screenshots as content), or save it to your **Private Cloud Space**, categorized (Business, Cooking, Travel, etc.) for future reference.

- If you ignore the button and select no option, **nothing happens**: the screenshot remains on your device as-is—it is not uploaded, copied, or viewed by us.

- Detection and button display occur **entirely locally on your device**. Identifying a new screenshot does not trigger an upload to our servers.

- **No screenshot is uploaded to our servers without an explicit action from you**—whether you choose to post it to the Timeline or save it to your Private Cloud Space. Unuploaded content stays on your device, unviewed and unstored by us.

## 2. Background Operations (Important Disclosure)
The Android operating system does not provide a standardized API for screenshot detection across all device models and manufacturers (some restrict access or utilize different storage paths). Therefore, for the application to function properly:

- **2.1.** The app operates a background service that monitors the addition of new screenshots to your device’s Screenshots folder.

- **2.2.** A persistent notification will display indicating that the app is running in the background. This is an Android transparency requirement allowing you to verify active service status at any time.

- **2.3.** The app may request an exemption from battery optimization constraints on certain devices (particularly Xiaomi, Huawei, Oppo, and Samsung models that automatically terminate background applications). This request is strictly to ensure uninterrupted screenshot detection. Granting this exemption is optional, but declining it may cause automatic detection to cease.

- **2.4.** Background service operations are strictly limited to monitoring new screenshot files. It **does not** read your messages, record your screen, capture screenshots independently, browse other files, or upload any data without your command.

- **2.5.** You can stop the background service at any time in the app settings. The app will continue to function with the loss of automatic detection only (requiring manual screenshot selection).

## 4. App Features & Privacy Levels
Akukas provides different workflows for managing your screenshots, each governed by distinct privacy rules:

## 4.1. Timeline Publishing (Public Content)
- Published content appears on the public Timeline—a global feed where users share screenshots of interest. It is visible to other users based on your chosen privacy settings (Public / Followers Only).

- Published content is subject to Content Guidelines outlined in the Terms and Conditions. Other users can interact with and share it.

- Public posts undergo automated AI filtering to detect potential privacy infringements (visible phone numbers, personal data, private chats) or policy violations, along with human review by our moderation team. **This filtering applies exclusively to publicly published content.**
