# Delete your Akukas account

You can delete your Akukas account and its data at any time. There are two ways to do it.

## From inside the app

1. Open **Akukas** and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Account & Security**.
3. Choose **Delete account** and confirm.

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately.

## By email

If you cannot sign in — for example you lost access to your account — email
**akukasapp@gmail.com** from the address registered to the account, with the subject
**"Account deletion request"**. Include your username so we can identify the account.

We will confirm the request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

## What gets deleted

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Account details — name, username, email, date of birth, country, profile photo | Deleted |
| Screenshots and posts you uploaded | Deleted, including the stored image files |
| Comments, likes, saves, reposts and collections | Deleted |
| Direct messages you sent | Deleted |
| Followers and following relationships | Deleted |
| Device registrations and push notification tokens | Deleted |

## What is kept, and for how long

- **A 30-day grace period.** Your account and content are removed from the app immediately, then
  permanently purged after 30 days. Contact us within that window if the deletion was a mistake.
- **Crash and diagnostic reports** are retained for up to 90 days and are anonymised — they are no
  longer linked to your account after deletion.
- **Records we are legally required to keep**, such as reports of abuse involving your account, may
  be retained where the law requires it.
